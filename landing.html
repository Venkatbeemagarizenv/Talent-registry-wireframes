Questions

Frequently asked

The essentials on how access, privacy, and the pipeline work.

How does the hiring pipeline work?
Every application advances through five stages — Applied, HR Reviewed, Forwarded, Interview, and Selected. Students see their own progress, HR sees everything, and enterprises see the candidates HR forwards to them, so no one is left guessing where things stand.
How do students sign in?
Students sign in with their existing ZenCube account through single sign-on (SSO). Their profile — projects, skills, and rubric scores — syncs automatically from ZenCube, so there’s nothing to re-enter.
How does an enterprise get started?
Enterprises register, wait for HR approval, then post openings. Once approved, recruiters can search talent, bookmark promising profiles with notes, and review the candidates HR forwards to them.
Will students’ contact details be shared with companies?
No. The model is HR-mediated by design: students and enterprises never exchange contact information directly. HR reviews fit, forwards the right people, and moderates every conversation in between.
What can recruiters see about a student?
Recruiters browse profiles — projects, skills, and rubric scores — but not private contact details unless contact permission has been explicitly granted through HR.
Is activity on the platform auditable?
Yes. Every HR and admin action is written to an immutable audit log — actor, change, and timestamp — and all access is governed by fine-grained, role-based permissions.
Secure access Students sign in with ZenCube SSO; HR and enterprises use email — all protected by role-based permissions.
